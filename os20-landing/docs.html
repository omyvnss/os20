WHY OS20

You’ve been choosing between easy and flexible. OS20 is the third option — with your own AI on top.

You’ve been choosing between software that’s easy to start but impossible to change, and software that’s flexible but takes months to set up. OS20 is the third option: a production-ready CRM you can reshape as you go — running on the AI models you already pay for.

What makes OS20 different

OS20 is a platform you can build on, not a product you configure.

Built for Agents

AI agents operate inside your data model with real permissions. Skills, Tools, MCP — every action is permission-aware, cited, and logged.

Bring Your Own AI

Connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, or local Ollama. No lock-in to one model vendor.

Modern Stack

React, TypeScript, Postgres. Your team already knows how to extend OS20. No proprietary languages, no gatekeeping.

No Lock-In

Open-source core, self-hostable, export your data anytime. Self-host or Cloud — same model, same API.

Who is OS20 for

⬢
Large enterprises replacing Salesforce

Your team spends more time fighting the tool than using it. Cost rises and lock-in deepens. OS20 gives you a buildable alternative that runs on your own AI.

⚡
Startups with technical founders

You’ve outgrown spreadsheets and Notion. You need a CRM that scales with your ambitions — and the model keys you already hold.

◈
GTM teams looking for an edge

Build your own lead scoring, enrichment, and outbound workflows — directly on the CRM that holds the truth.

◉
Privacy-conscious organizations

Self-host and own your data end to end. Regulatory, contractual, or principle — your VPC, your Postgres.

▦
Salesforce partners & web agencies

Know TypeScript/React/Postgres? You already know OS20. No APEX, no certifications — ship faster at lower license cost.

Who OS20 is not for

—
Teams that want a CRM they never have to think about

OS20 rewards teams that shape their tools over time. If you want fully managed, Pipedrive or HubSpot will serve you well.

∞
Companies that need hundreds of pre-built integrations today

The ecosystem is growing fast but not yet Salesforce-broad. Comfortable building what’s missing? You’ll love it.

◎
Teams that insist on one AI vendor

We’re Bring-Your-Own-Key by design. If you need OS20 to force a single model provider, it’s a fit only if that’s your own choice.
